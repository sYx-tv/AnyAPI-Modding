# AnyEngineParts 1.0.2

AnyEngineParts bundle 1.0.2 (AnyEngineParts 0.2.3): the engine parts turn up in survival world loot about a quarter
less often than the game's own parts of the same tier.

- **Before:** the Turbo 3x3 and the small supercharger, wastegate, blow-off valve and air filter were half as common
  as the stock parts of their tier. The Turbo 6x6, the large parts, the intercoolers, the Rotary and the Dyno were as
  common as the stock parts of their tier or more.
- **Tiers are unchanged:** the small parts drop after the 2nd bunker (tech tier 3), and the rest after the 3rd (tech
  tier 4). They are mechanical loot, found in industrial, construction, aircraft and bunker loot spots.
- The GPS Sensor keeps the stock sensors' rate. EngineSound 0.4.1 and GpsPart 1.0.4 are unchanged.

Published untested in game, at Nate's request. For Anymaker 0.1.27 with AnyAPI 0.40.1 or newer. See
[Turbo](../mods/turbo.md#getting-it), [Rotary](../mods/rotary.md#getting-it) and [Dyno](../mods/dyno.md#getting-it).
