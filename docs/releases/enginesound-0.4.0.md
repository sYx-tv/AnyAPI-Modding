# EngineSound 0.4.0

**Real engine sounds.** Engines no longer play pitched game recordings. Each sound is generated live from the
engine's cylinders firing in their real order, through their own exhaust banks, like the AnyEngineParts rotary.

- Inline, V and boxer engines each get five sounds of their own in the Properties Tool (inline: Stock, Sport,
  Street tuner, Race, Diesel; V: Stock, Muscle, Exotic, Race, Truck diesel; boxer: Stock, Rally rumble,
  Air-cooled, Race, Diesel), plus the game's three sounds and Custom.
- The cylinder count follows the engine's stretch, from a single up to 16 cylinders, and bigger blocks sound
  deeper.
- Starter crank, idle lope, rev limiter bounce, overrun pops, intake roar, valvetrain and diesel clatter.
- The Engine sound panel names the engine ("V8 (medium block): Muscle"), lists its sounds to click, and has a new
  Custom page: exhaust note, bark, rasp, muffler, cam lope, pops, intake, valvetrain, firing/headers, diesel and
  volume.

Sounds saved with older versions open at the same slider value; old Custom tunes sound different and may need
picking again. Guide: [EngineSound](../mods/engine-sound.md).
