# AnyMirror 1.0.0 and AnyBalance 1.2.0

**AnyMirror** is a new mod for building symmetric creations. With either edge tool equipped and mirroring on, every edge you place is placed a second time, mirrored across a plane through your build. A see-through mirror wall shows where the plane is, and a badge at the top of the screen shows what is on. Keys (rebindable in Mod Controls): `J` mirror on/off, `K` wall on/off, `L` cycle the axis, `[` and `]` nudge the plane, `\` re-centre it. Wall colour and opacity are in Mod Settings. AnyMirror uses the experimental SDK and only activates on Anymaker 0.1.23 / Steam build 25755694. Guide: [AnyMirror](../mods/mirror.md).

**AnyBalance 1.2.0** adds the creation's size (X by Z, then height) to its summary card, under the mass. Guide: [AnyBalance](../mods/balance.md).

Both mods built and passed their tests on Windows, and the author confirmed them in game before release.
