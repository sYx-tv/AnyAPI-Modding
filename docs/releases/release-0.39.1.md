# AnyAPI 0.39.1 and AnyGraphics bundle 1.0.2

For Anymaker 0.1.25 (Steam build 25904830).

- **AnyAPI 0.39.1:** lamp beams are switched off on AMD graphics cards, where they blew out the picture. Adds the
  `anyapi.gpu_info` service ([GPU info](../api/gpu-info.md)).
- **AnyGraphics bundle 1.0.2** (AnyGraphics 0.31.2 with ThirdPerson): hides the lamp beam settings on AMD cards, and
  carries AnyGraphics 0.31.1's photo-mode fix: the free camera no longer spins with third person on.

The fix was confirmed by a player on an AMD card before release.
